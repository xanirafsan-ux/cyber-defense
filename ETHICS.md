# Ethical Use

All analysis in this repository is intended for learning and authorized defensive work.

- Use only synthetic data, personal systems, or explicitly authorized labs.
- Do not scan, exploit, or test bKash, employers, customer systems, or third-party infrastructure.
- Never commit passwords, tokens, private keys, personal data, customer data, or confidential logs.
- Sanitize screenshots and packet captures before sharing.
- Treat detections as hypotheses until validated by authorized evidence.
